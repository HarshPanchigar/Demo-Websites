Our Medical Services

From routine preventative care to advanced diagnostics, we provide complete family healthcare under one roof.

Adult General Medicine

Proactive, preventative health solutions to optimize adult health across all stages of life. We focus on treating the root cause, not just the symptoms.

  • Annual Physicals Comprehensive biometric assessments and lab work to catch issues early.
  • Chronic Care Management Long-term treatment strategies for hypertension, diabetes, and cholesterol.
  • Acute Illness Fast, effective treatment for sudden flus, infections, and minor injuries.

Pediatric Care

A warm, inclusive setting focused on gentle acute care and long-term developmental wellness for your growing children.

  • Child Immunizations Safe, timely completion of all required and recommended vaccines.
  • Developmental Tracking Monitoring physical, emotional, and cognitive growth milestones.
  • Rapid Sick Visits Immediate, comforting care for high fevers, earaches, and common childhood illnesses.

On-Site Diagnostics & Labs

We eliminate the friction of external referrals. Receive high-quality tests right here in our clinic, drastically accelerating your time-to-diagnosis.

  • Comprehensive Blood Work Complete Blood Count (CBC), lipid panels, and thyroid testing.
  • Digital X-Ray Advanced, low-radiation imaging for respiratory and bone issue assessments.
  • Point-of-Care Testing Rapid results for Flu, Strep A, and other immediate infectious diagnostic needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We are proud to welcome new patients for both adult and pediatric care. You can easily schedule your first consultation online or by calling our front desk.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We accept walk-ins for minor urgent care needs based on our daily availability. However, patients with scheduled appointments are always given priority, so we highly recommend booking ahead if possible.

How do I get my lab results?

Routine lab results are uploaded to our secure patient portal within 48 hours of your test. If any immediate medical action or medication adjustment is required, our clinical team will call you directly.
